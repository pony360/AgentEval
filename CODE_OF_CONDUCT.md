# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and give constructive feedback.
Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated in any project space.

**Reporting.** Email the maintainers at **CONDUCT_CONTACT_EMAIL** (replace with a real address before
publishing). All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate,
including warnings or removal from the project.
